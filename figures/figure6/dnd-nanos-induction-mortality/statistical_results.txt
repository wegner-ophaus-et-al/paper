Statistical analysis: % severe or dead by condition
=======================================================

Each condition has n=3 repeats. scipy.stats.normaltest (used by utils.parametric) requires at least 8 samples per group and raises an error below that, so normality cannot be formally assessed here. All comparisons below therefore use the non-parametric Mann-Whitney U test unconditionally, and an omnibus Kruskal-Wallis test is used in place of a one-way ANOVA.
Caveat: with n=3 vs n=3, the exact two-sided Mann-Whitney U test has a minimum possible p-value of 0.1, so no pairwise comparison here can reach the conventional p<0.05 threshold. Exact p-values are reported below rather than relying on significance stars alone.
